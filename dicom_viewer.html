Free Online DICOM Viewer, DICOM to PDF & DICOM to JPG Converter

RAD-XR is a free browser-based DICOM viewer that runs entirely on your device. No installation, no signup, no file upload — your medical images never leave your computer. Open CT, MRI, X-ray, Ultrasound, Mammography, PET, and other DICOM studies, and download them as an A4 PDF report or a high-resolution JPG image.

✅ Supported DICOM Transfer Syntaxes

  • Uncompressed — Implicit VR Little Endian, Explicit VR Little Endian, Explicit VR Big Endian
  • JPEG Baseline (Process 1)
  • JPEG Extended (Process 2 & 4)
  • JPEG Lossless (Process 14 & 14 SV1)
  • JPEG-LS Lossless and Near-Lossless
  • JPEG 2000 Lossless and Lossy
  • RLE Lossless

🛠️ Features

  • 2D slice-by-slice DICOM viewer with slider, keyboard and mouse-wheel navigation
  • 3D volume rendering for multi-slice CT studies
  • Brightness, contrast, invert and Window Width / Window Level tools
  • Export A4 PDF report (WhatsApp-ready format)
  • Export high-resolution JPG (3K+ pixels wide)
  • Multi-frame DICOM support
  • 100% private — no server upload, works offline after page load

📖 How to Use

  1. Drag & drop your .dcm files, or click Choose DICOM Files.
  2. Browse slices using the navigator. Use the tools panel to adjust brightness, contrast, and window level.
  3. Click Download PDF for a printable A4 medical report, or Download JPG for a high-resolution image.
