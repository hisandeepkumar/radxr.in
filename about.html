⚕️ One Platform. Every Report. Delivered.

RAD‑XR is a DICOM PACS and radiology workflow software platform built for radiology departments, diagnostic centres and imaging clinics. It quietly runs in the background, listens for DICOM studies from X‑ray, MRI and CT machines, groups images by patient, and turns them into clean, print‑ready PDF reports plus rich, interactive HTML reports.

The moment a study is complete, RAD‑XR delivers both files to your team on Telegram and WhatsApp — no manual exports, no USB drives, no waiting. The HTML report opens in any phone or PC's native browser, letting the reader adjust brightness, contrast, slide through images, and even explore CT studies in 3D.

RAD‑XR was founded, created, and developed by Sandeep Yadav. The official website is radxr.in.

⚙️ How It Works

Four simple steps. Zero manual work.

STEP 1

Study Arrives

An X‑ray, MRI or CT modality sends the DICOM study to RAD‑XR over the network — or a file appears in the watched folder.

STEP 2

Smart Grouping

RAD‑XR groups all images of the same patient and waits a short moment for the full study to arrive.

STEP 3

Reports Built

A polished multi‑page PDF and an interactive HTML report are generated automatically, with patient metadata on each page.

STEP 4

Delivered & Archived

Both files are sent directly to Telegram and WhatsApp, and safely stored in the searchable history for future resends.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about RAD‑XR, its features, security, and how credits work.

What is RAD‑XR?

RAD‑XR is a DICOM PACS and radiology workflow software that receives studies from X‑ray, MRI, CT and other modalities, generates PDF and interactive HTML reports, and delivers them through Telegram and WhatsApp — fully automatically and securely.

Who created RAD‑XR?

RAD‑XR was created by Sandeep Yadav, who is also its founder and lead developer.

Which file types does RAD‑XR deliver?

RAD‑XR delivers two file types per study: a print‑ready PDF report and an interactive HTML report. The HTML file opens in any native browser on mobile or PC — no app, no install — and works offline.

Can I adjust brightness and contrast in the report?

Yes. The interactive HTML report lets you adjust brightness and contrast (window/level) in real time for clearer, more accurate viewing.

Can I slide through the images?

Yes. The HTML report lets you scroll or swipe through every image of the study smoothly — like a filmstrip — on both phone and desktop.

Is CT 3D view available?

Yes. The interactive HTML report includes a CT 3D view — rotate, zoom and inspect CT studies from any angle, right inside the browser.

How do credits work?

Each successful report delivery uses one credit. Telegram and WhatsApp have separate balances, so you only spend on the channel you actually use. Credits never expire.

How do I recharge credits?

Recharge is handled personally by the founder. Contact Sandeep Yadav at +91 9588027038 or sandeepydvktp@gmail.com to top up your account.

Is RAD‑XR secure?

Yes. RAD‑XR uses end-to-end encryption and delivers reports directly from your system to the recipient. No cloud server or middle service is involved.

Does RAD‑XR use any cloud service?

No. RAD‑XR runs entirely on your local system. There is no external cloud storage and no third‑party intermediary handling your patient data.

Which modalities does RAD‑XR support?

RAD‑XR supports DICOM studies from X‑ray, MRI, CT scan and other imaging modalities.

Does RAD‑XR support DICOM?

Yes. RAD‑XR receives DICOM studies over the network from any standard modality and also monitors an archive folder for files added by other systems.

Can reports be sent on WhatsApp?

Yes. RAD‑XR supports WhatsApp Business delivery, routing each report to the number associated with the study's accession.

Who is RAD‑XR designed for?

Radiology departments, diagnostic and imaging centres, hospitals, and healthcare IT teams that handle DICOM studies every day.

What is the official website of RAD‑XR?

The official website of RAD‑XR is radxr.in.
