What is RAD-XR?

RAD-XR is DICOM PACS and radiology workflow software designed for receiving DICOM medical images, managing patient studies, generating PDF reports, and supporting digital radiography and radiology workflows.

RAD-XR provides a DICOM receiver, medical imaging gateway, report generation tools, and workflow features for medical imaging centers, diagnostic centers, hospitals, and radiology environments.

The official website of RAD-XR is radxr.in.
